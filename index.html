2 · Hear the tones

Frequency shift keying = sending data by switching between tones. Play the two markers and drag the slider to hear the alphabet the modem uses. Watch these show up on the Spectrum tab.

3 · Send a message over sound

Type something and hit Send — it plays as tones. Point a second phone (also on this page, mic enabled) nearby and watch it decode. Same device also hears itself, so you can test solo first.

Both phones must pick the same modulation. FSK & FHSS are the robust ones; ASK is fragile on purpose; Chirp is experimental.

Constellation (I/Q)

Select DBPSK in Messenger and send a message. Each symbol's phase change plots here: a point near bit 0 (right) means "same phase as last symbol", near bit 1 (left) means "flipped". Tight clusters = clean link; a smeared ring = the room scrambling the phase. Best solo/loopback.
